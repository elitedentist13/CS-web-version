Extraction index · 拔牙倾向

Chairside reminder of what the Banana ceph score is looking at. It is a tracing vs the selected norm set — not a decision to extract.

Do not extract from this number alone. Crowding, Bolton, growth, TMJ, smile and the patient’s wish are not on the lateral film.

What the score is

Starts at 50 (undecided). Each measure that is 1–2 SD from the chosen HK Chinese or Caucasian mean tilts the score. 2 SD tilts more. Soft-tissue millimetres count only after the film ruler is calibrated.

Use HK Chinese (Chan 1972; Cooke & Wei 1988) for this clinic. Caucasian IMPA 90° and lips on the S-line will call many Southern Chinese faces “extract” when they are only the local norm.

Bands

Low 0–34 Keep / IPR / expansion more likely from the ceph.
Borderline 35–54 Mixed. Models and smile decide.
Moderate 55–74 Extraction camouflage is on the table.
High 75–100 Typical bimax / convex / lip-protrusive pattern.

Extract or keep — from the tracing

Leans extract · 倾向拔牙

  • IMPA high — lower incisors already proclined; little room to unravel crowding without making the lip worse.
  • Interincisal acute — bimaxillary protrusion (common in HK).
  • FMIA low — Tweed: lower incisor too proclined relative to Frankfort.
  • Convex profile — A-point ahead of N–Pog; retraction can flatten the face.
  • Lips ahead of Steiner S-line (Ls / Li to Sn–Pog′) — after calibration.
  • Class II ANB / Wits — scored as upper-arch camouflage (premolars more often than four premolars).

Leans keep · 倾向不拔

  • IMPA low / retroclined — further retraction dumps the incisors and hollows the lip.
  • Interincisal obtuse — not a bimax face.
  • FMIA high — Tweed already “upright”.
  • Concave profile / strong chin — extraction can make the face dish-in.
  • Retrusive lips on the S-line — do not retract for “numbers”.
  • High-angle (SN–GoGn out) — small penalty: retraction needs vertical control; not an automatic extract.

Class II vs Class III — not the same extraction

  • Class II adds to the score as upper-arch camouflage. Typical pattern is upper premolars (sometimes two uppers only), not automatic 14-24-34-44.
  • Class III is not added as a 4-premolar extract. The hint says lower-arch camouflage or surgery. Retroclined lower incisors in Class III usually argue against more lower retraction.

What is missing (so the index stays humble)

  • Arch-length discrepancy / crowding / spacing
  • Bolton, tooth-size, unerupted 8s
  • Growth remaining, FMA vs age, airway / tonsils
  • TMJ, periodontal support, restorations, missing teeth
  • Smile arc, gingival display, nasolabial angle on the photo

If those conflict with a High score, trust the mouth and the face, not the chip.
